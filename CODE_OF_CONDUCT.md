# Code of conduct

TermLook is a small open source project, and everyone who takes part is expected to
keep it a respectful, harassment-free place. This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good intent.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experience levels and ways of working.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email,
  without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban
anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@T0ks1k24](https://github.com/T0ks1k24)
privately through GitHub, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment
itself. Reports are kept confidential, and the reporter's identity is not shared.
